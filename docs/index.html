    A FEW USEFUL ANSWERS

    Before you begin.

    Can I register without experience or a CV?

    Yes. Freshers, trainees and experienced candidates are welcome. A CV and an email address are optional. A working mobile number is required.

    What kind of vendors can register?

    Independent service providers, contractors, manufacturers and suppliers: maintenance, fabrication, manpower, transport, packaging, raw materials, IT, catering, security and more.

    Can I use my LinkedIn profile?

    You can add your LinkedIn profile link. Automatic profile import is not enabled; your work history and skills need to be entered in the form.

    How will my information be used?

    With your consent, your details go to the association’s organiser and may be shared with relevant members for recruitment or business enquiries. FormSubmit processes the form and any attachment to deliver them by email. Registration details are also stored in the association organiser’s private Google Sheet. Contact ecotech12ia@gmail.com to correct your details or request deletion.
